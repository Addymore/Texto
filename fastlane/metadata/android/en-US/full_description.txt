Texto is an SMS/MMS messenger by Addymore, based on QUIK and QKSMS.

• Personal themes: accent palettes, custom colors, light/dark/AMOLED appearance, conversation cards and adjustable spacing.
• Reachable navigation with message totals and unread indicators.
• Private conversations protected by a PIN or supported device biometrics. Number rules keep future messages private too.
• Archive, contact profiles, scheduled messages, delayed sending, search and backups.
• Local spam rules for numbers, prefixes and phrases, trusted exceptions and a blocklist.
• A PIN-protected recycle bin with restore and 30, 60 or 90-day retention.
• MMS attachments, group messaging and dual-SIM support where provided by the device and carrier.

Set Texto as your default SMS app to send and receive messages. Carrier charges may apply. SMS delivery reports depend on your carrier; RCS and SMS read receipts are not supported.

Private space is app-level protection, not encryption of Android's shared message storage. Other SMS-authorized apps and exported backups are outside this protection. Clearing app data removes local privacy and recycle-bin rules. Android may defer automatic cleanup.

Optional integrations can open separately installed proprietary blocking apps; Texto's own blocking rules work without them.

The F-Droid application uses app.texto.sms. It installs separately from the GitHub development preview (app.texto.sms.debug). Saved themes, PINs and privacy rules do not migrate automatically between these installations.
